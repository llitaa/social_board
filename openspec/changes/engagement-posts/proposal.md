# Proposal: engagement posts that sell (30.10.2026)

## Why
The personal posts so far position Anton for employers. Anton's goal now: an AlpiType client or an AI-director role, from one audience: founders and owners without a technical background who have bought or commissioned software and need someone to carry the technology, plus the people who hire that role. Text posts about C++ do not reach them; a diagram they can place themselves on does.

## Unfair advantage (what the posts sell, stated once here, never in a post)
1. He has carried production in industries where failure costs lives or millions (medtech, defence, semiconductor, aviation) and still writes code with agents every day. Vendors are either talkers or coders; the founder gets both in one person.
2. He wrote the operating model for AI with liability (70/30: who trains, who controls, who is accountable). A founder buys judgement with a written method, not a model.
3. Numbers from his own hands: up to 30% runtime in a legacy code base, six developers to technical production readiness in five months, a team from one to seven.
4. He runs his own marketing on the system he sells (board, voice memos, agents, 70/30 approvals). Showing it is proof and entertainment at once.
5. Nothing to resell: code lives in the client's repository, on-premise where needed, exit built in.
Weak spots, honestly: no public big-logo AI reference, the workshop was never delivered, the personal profile is detached from AlpiType. Conversion therefore runs through Anton as a person, not through the company page.

## What changes
- One series, "Nach dem Go-live", numbered episodes, Monday and Friday. Each episode is one diagram in the house style with a three-line caption and one question that makes the reader locate himself on the chart.
- Entertainment comes from naming: Tal der Ausnahmen, Friedhof der Piloten, Teure PowerPoint, Glück. Recurring characters: the pitch, the demo, the person who answers the phone after go-live.
- Flow: Anton gives a topic (one sentence) → two candidates (diagram kind, title, labels, caption) → yes/no → render → draft on the board. No model in the background.
- Conversion path: post → comment or DM → 30-minute call with an engineer → a fixed-price Betriebs-Check (one week: read the code and the pilot, name who carries the exceptions, list what operations still needs). The check is the product the series sells; the call is free. Comments get a one-line answer and, where it fits, "ich schicke Ihnen die Checkliste per DM" (one page, to be written).
- Measurement: comments and DMs per episode, not impressions. An episode that brings no comment in 48 hours gets a follow-up comment by Anton with the sharpest line.

## Out of scope
Company-page posts (stay EN, stay as they are), the photo posts on Wednesday, the Zürich job search.
