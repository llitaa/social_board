The day a client stops working with us, nothing stops running. The code is in their repository, the data is on their servers, and the automation keeps doing its 70 percent.

That is the test we build against. Industrial or office, the automation we write is code inside the client's own environment: their Microsoft 365, or software next to the machine, on-premise. Not a platform they rent access to.

What the client keeps:

The source. Every rule, every exception path, every integration is readable and versioned in their own repository. The next request is a change in code, not a ticket to a vendor.

The data. It stays where it is produced. Nothing is copied into a third-party platform to be processed there.

The exit. If they want to continue with their own team or with someone else, they can. That is a property of the architecture, not a clause in a contract.

The 30 percent stays theirs as well. Exceptions go to a person on their side who approves them, and who trains, who controls, who is accountable stays written down.

If your automation ended with its subscription tomorrow, what would you still have?
