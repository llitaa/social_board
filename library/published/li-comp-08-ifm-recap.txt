Two days at the Internationales Forum Mechatronik in Memmingen, and the conversations are still running. Thank you, Bayern Innovativ and the Cluster Mechatronik & Automation, for a well-organised event.

The stand did its job. While the team was in conversations, the roll-up explained who we are: control software, machine vision, predictive maintenance. The books on the table ran out. Some copies are now on their way to our clients by post.

The level was high. Real innovation in mechatronics, strong talks, and conversations that reached expert depth within minutes.

One pattern came up again and again. Most discussions already implied a software solution, and often one built on the output of a prompt. For some areas that is fine. For production it is not. Where the goal is production optimisation, a language model alone optimises nothing. The bigger task, and the one that pays, is mechatronic: automating the manual parts of the process with software that runs where the machines run.

The equation behind every one of these projects is the same: domain expertise, production know-how, and digital solutions that are actually capable of the task. The first two exist in the plant. The third is our part: shaping the digital solution so it is the most efficient one for the line, not the most fashionable one. Architecture, control and HMI software in C++/Qt, computer vision, AI where it earns its place, on-premise and at the edge. With technical responsibility after go-live.

We look forward to continuing the conversations from Memmingen and to contributing to measurable improvements in industrial systems.
